1 Introduction & Overview

Welcome to ASSAM STUDY HUB ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application titled ASSAM STUDY HUB (Package Name: com.aistudio.assamstudyhub.eduhub), designed to deliver structured educational content, textbook solutions, chapter summaries, and learning resources for students in Classes 1 through 12 following the Assam State Board (SEBA / AHSEC) and Asom Jatiya Bidyalay (AJB) curricula.

We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and digital well-being of all learners, educators, and guardians who use our platform. This document describes the specific types of information processed by the application, how that data is used, the security measures we employ, and the rights you retain over your information.

2 Information We Process

To provide a personalized learning experience and maintain individual student progress, the app processes only the minimum necessary information:

  • Student Profile Credentials: When setting up an account or profile, the user provides a display name, contact mobile number (used for unique account identification and verification), and the currently selected academic class/standard (Classes 1–12).
  • Academic Preferences: Selected curriculum board (e.g., Assam State Board or Asom Jatiya Bidyalay), chosen medium of instruction (Assamese Medium or English Medium), and selected academic subjects.
  • Local Learning Data: Bookmarked chapters, favorite study materials, offline reading states, and personal notes. These are stored locally in the application's sandboxed SQLite/Room database on the user's Android device.
Important Disclosure: ASSAM STUDY HUB does NOT request or collect sensitive personal data such as precise GPS location, biometric identifiers, physical address, financial account numbers, or microphone/camera recordings.

3 Device & Technical Diagnostics

When you run the application on an Android device, standard system-level technical telemetry may be processed solely to ensure app stability, performance, and compatibility across Android versions (Android 7.0 / API 24 through Android 16 / API 36):

  • Operating System & Architecture: Android OS version, device manufacturer, screen density, and architecture.
  • Network State: Verification of active internet connectivity via the standard ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permission to determine whether educational content can be streamed or updated.
  • Application Performance Data: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic crash reports and ANR (Application Not Responding) metrics generated during abnormal app termination.

4 Internet & Network Connectivity Requirements

ASSAM STUDY HUB operates as an internet-connected educational application. The app requires an active, validated internet connection (via Wi-Fi or cellular mobile data) to:

  • Fetch real-time educational curriculum, syllabus updates, and chapter explanations.
  • Verify valid session credentials and protect academic materials against unauthorized modifications.
  • Synchronize designated learning assets and announcements.

The application actively tests network reachability upon startup and throughout active sessions. If network loss occurs, the app gracefully notifies the student to reconnect, preventing corrupt data states or incomplete lesson downloads.

5 Advertising & Google AdMob

To maintain free and accessible educational content for students across Assam, the application is intended to display non-intrusive advertisements served through Google AdMob.

  • Third-Party Ad Serving: Google AdMob may process standard mobile advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID) and approximate coarse location signals to deliver relevant, safe, and family-appropriate advertisements.
  • Child-Directed Content Compliance: For educational sections designated for younger students, ad requests are configured in compliance with Google's Families Policy and applicable children's privacy regulations (such as requesting non-personalized ads and tags for child-directed treatment).
  • Ad Preferences: Users and parents can opt out of personalized interest-based advertising at any time through their Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID).
  • For more details on how Google processes advertising data, please review the Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

6 Firebase & Google Cloud Infrastructure

The application integrates certified backend services provided by Google Firebase (Firebase Project: assam-study-hub-3fef7) to ensure secure delivery and resource protection:

  • Firebase App Check: Employs reCAPTCHA Enterprise and device attestation tokens to verify that incoming API requests originate strictly from the authentic ASSAM STUDY HUB binary, preventing bot abuse, unauthorized scraping, and server impersonation.
  • Firebase AI Services: Utilized where enabled to assist in structured curriculum structuring and educational query assistance under enterprise privacy terms.
  • Google Services Engine: Configured via Google Services infrastructure for secure client-to-server communication.

Firebase infrastructure processes telemetry strictly as a data processor adhering to Google Cloud's security standards and ISO/IEC 27001 certifications.

7 How We Use Information

We use the processed information strictly for legitimate educational objectives:

  • Curriculum Delivery: Customizing the syllabus view according to the student's selected class (Classes 1–12), board, and preferred language medium.
  • Session Management: Retaining student sign-in state across app launches so learners can continue reading where they left off.
  • Offline Capability: Maintaining offline access to bookmarked textbook chapters within the device's local database.
  • Service Integrity: Detecting and mitigating malicious traffic, service abuse, and unauthorized access attempts.

8 Information Sharing & Disclosure

We maintain a strict zero-sale principle: ASSAM STUDY HUB does not sell, trade, lease, or commercially transfer user information to third parties. Information may only be disclosed under the following narrowly defined conditions:

  • Service Providers: Cloud infrastructure, security verification (Google Firebase), and advertising distribution (Google AdMob), all bound by strict confidentiality and data-processing obligations.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by applicable law, court order, or official governmental directive under applicable Indian laws (such as the Information Technology Act and Digital Personal Data Protection Act).
  • Safety & Protection: When deemed necessary to protect the safety, security, or legal rights of students, the public, or ASSAM STUDY HUB against digital threats.

9 Data Security & Storage

We employ robust industry-standard technical and organizational security controls to protect student data:

  • Encrypted Transmission: All data transmissions between the application and backend endpoints are secured via Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Device-Level Sandboxing: User preferences, saved notes, and progress records are maintained in an application-private Room SQLite database that cannot be accessed by other unauthorized apps installed on your device.
  • Access Governance: Backend access is governed by strict role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication, and Firebase App Check validation.

10 Data Retention & Erasure

We retain student profile records only for as long as the account remains active and necessary to provide educational services.

  • Local device data (bookmarks, study history, cached notes) can be cleared at any time by clearing the application storage in Android settings (Settings > Apps > ASSAM STUDY HUB > Storage > Clear Data).
  • Server-side account records (mobile number and display profile) may be deleted upon verified request by emailing our support desk at lkbro781136@gmail.com.
